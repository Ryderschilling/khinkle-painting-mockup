FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Still curious about something? Give us a call and we will walk you through it.

(760) 360-2424
Can I hire you to paint my entire home?

Yes. We do custom interior painting for your entire home, along with exterior painting and any repairs that are needed. We would be happy to schedule a time to give you an estimate.

How quickly can I get an estimate?

Estimates are scheduled and provided within the same week, if not the next business day. Give us a call to schedule your appointment.

What painting services does K. Hinkle offer?

We specialize in whole house painting including custom interior and exterior painting, concrete coatings, cabinet finishes, stained finishes and false graining.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free for all of our services, including interior painting and concrete coatings.

Do you do complete interior and exterior work?

Yes. We take on a full range of interior and exterior painting projects, whether they are big or small.

Do you do new concrete work?

Yes. Beyond painting, we handle concrete coating projects: epoxy, metallic, chip coating, deck coatings, stamped overlays and stains.

Do you do stucco and drywall repair?

Yes. Our team handles minor stucco and drywall repair, plus acoustic and popcorn ceiling removal, as part of your painting job.
